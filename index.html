OpenCode MCP Integrations

A personal, single-user tool. Not a product, not a service, and not offered to the public.

What this is

This OAuth client exists to let one person's local OpenCode installation talk to their own Google account. Two local MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers running on that person's own computer use it:

  • a Gmail server, to read and label the owner's mail;
  • a Google Calendar server, to read the owner's calendars and events.

Both servers run locally on the owner's machine. There is no hosted backend, no shared service, and no account to sign up for. The OAuth client exists solely so that the local processes can be authorized by, and call, Google's APIs.

What this is not

  • It does not collect, sell, or share data with anyone.
  • It does not have analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not have a server that stores your mail or calendar data.
  • It does not accept new users. It has exactly one user: its owner.

Scopes requested

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify — read, label, and organize the owner's mail
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.settings.basic — read and manage the owner's mail settings and filters
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar — read the owner's calendars and events

The app asks for nothing beyond what those local servers need to answer the owner's own requests, and it never runs without an explicit action from its owner.

Full detail on what is accessed, where it goes, and how to revoke access: Privacy Policy.
